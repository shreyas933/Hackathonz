# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. What is Brightloop SMS?
Brightloop Subscription Management System (SMS) is an end-to-end platform for managing customer subscription lifecycles, quotations with mandatory Admin approvals, recurring billing, and multi-scenario payment reconciliation.

### 2. Can Sales Representatives send quotations directly to customers?
No. All draft quotations require mandatory Admin review and authorization before they can be sent to or accepted by the customer.

### 3. How does the system prevent duplicate billing?
The recurring billing engine enforces idempotency keys based on the subscription identifier and the specific billing period window.
